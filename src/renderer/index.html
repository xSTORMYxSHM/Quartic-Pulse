ADD IT TO OBSWINDOW CAPTURE
  1. Open OBS Output above.
  2. In OBS Sources, press + and choose Window Capture.
  3. Select Quartic Pulse — OBS Output.
  4. Turn off Capture Cursor, then fit it to the canvas.
  5. For green screen, add OBS Chroma Key to the source. Quartic Pulse automatically keeps key-green out of the fractal.
